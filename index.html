Tips & Tricks of the Trade

A bit of free advice from your local expert.

Mistake #1: Scrubbing Stains

Scrubbing a fresh spill is the worst thing you can do. It pushes the stain deeper and can damage fibres.

Mistake #2: Supermarket Sprays

“Miracle” sprays can bleach or leave sticky residue that attracts dirt.

Mistake #3: Waiting Too Long

The longer a stain sits, the harder it is to remove. Call sooner for best results.
