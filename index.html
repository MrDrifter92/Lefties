Tips & Tricks of the Trade

Mistake #1: Scrubbing Stains

Scrubbing pushes the stain deeper and can damage fibres. Blot gently with a clean, white cloth.

Mistake #2: Supermarket Sprays

Many contain bleaching agents or sticky detergents that attract dirt and make the problem worse.

Mistake #3: Waiting Too Long

The longer it sits, the harder it is to remove. Coffee and pet urine can permanently dye fibres.
