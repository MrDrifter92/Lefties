Pro-Tips from the Trade

A bit of free advice to save you from common carpet care mistakes.

Mistake #1: Scrubbing Stains

Scrubbing a fresh spill is the worst thing you can do. It pushes the stain deeper and damages the fibres. Always blot gently with a clean, white cloth, working from the outside in.

Mistake #2: Supermarket Sprays

Those "miracle" sprays often contain bleaching agents or harsh detergents that leave a sticky residue, attracting more dirt over time. They can make a professional's job much harder.

Mistake #3: Waiting Too Long

The longer a stain sits, the harder it is to remove. Acidic stains from coffee, wine, or pet urine can permanently dye carpet fibres. For best results, call us sooner rather than later.
