The craft & method

Controlled cleaning. No shortcuts.

Every job runs the same process: identify the fibre, test for colourfastness, treat with the right pH, then extract properly so carpets and lounges dry in hours, not days. No guessing, no one-size-fits-all chemical.

  • Fabric-matched chemistry. Wool, synthetics, linen and delicate lounge fabrics get treated differently — because they behave differently.
  • High-recovery extraction. Moisture gets pulled back out properly, not left to soak in.

Good to know

Common questions.

How long will my carpets take to dry?

Usually 4 to 6 hours. It depends on airflow, humidity and how thick the carpet is. Running fans or the air con speeds it up noticeably.

Do I need to move all my furniture?

Just the small stuff — breakables, electronics, anything on the floor. We can clean around the big items like beds and entertainment units.

Are your products safe for kids and pets?

Yes. We use professional-grade products that are non-toxic and residue-free once dry. If anyone in the house has allergies or sensitivities, tell us and we'll adjust.

Can you guarantee every stain comes out?

No honest cleaner can promise that. Some substances permanently dye carpet fibres. What we do guarantee is the right training, the right products, and a straight answer about what's realistic before we start.

How much does it cost?

It depends on room count, fabric type and how much treatment the job needs. Quotes are free and there's no obligation — send a photo and you'll get an accurate number fast.

Do you clean wool carpets and rugs?

Yes. Wool and other natural fibres need pH-neutral, WoolSafe-approved chemistry and careful moisture control. That's standard practice here, not an upsell.
