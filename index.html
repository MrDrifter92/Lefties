Tips & Tricks of the Trade

A bit of free advice from your local expert.

Mistake #1: Scrubbing Stains

Scrubbing pushes the stain deeper and can damage fibres. Blot gently with a clean, white cloth.

Mistake #2: Supermarket Sprays

Many contain bleaching agents or sticky detergents that attract dirt. They often make it worse.

Mistake #3: Waiting Too Long

Coffee and pet urine can permanently dye fibres — call sooner than later.
