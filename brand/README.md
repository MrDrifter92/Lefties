| Folder | Mark | Date | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `logo-kit-230826/` | Wave bubble | 23 Aug 2026 | **Newest.** Complete kit, built from Adam's own artwork |

## Heads-up: the live site does not match the newest kit

`assets/img/logo.png` and `logo-reverse.png` are the **droplet** lockup from
the 21 Aug pack. The 23 Aug kit in `logo-kit-230826/` uses a different mark —
a **wave bubble** — and adds an "Upholstery & Fabric Revival" subtitle the
droplet pack does not have.

These are two different identities, not two exports of one. Whichever is
chosen, the site should be switched over deliberately:

- to adopt the wave bubble, replace `assets/img/logo.png` with
  `logo-kit-230826/lefties-horizontal.png` and `assets/img/logo-reverse.png`
  with `lefties-horizontal-white.png`, then re-check the header and footer
- to keep the droplet, `logo-kit-230826/` stays as reference only

Nothing has been switched automatically.

## Common to both

Still raster, not vector. See `BRAND.md` section 1 and the closing note in
`logo-kit-230826/READ-ME.txt` — both recommend getting a true vector redraw
before signage, vehicle wrap or embroidery.
