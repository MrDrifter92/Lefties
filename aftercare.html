Aftercare

The next 24 hours.

A clean is only half the job. Here's how to get the best result out of the drying window and keep the finish for longer.

Home/Aftercare
Straight after

While it dries.

  1. Give it airflow

    Open windows, run ceiling fans, or put the air con on dry mode. Expect 4 to 6 hours for carpet, up to 8 for thick rugs and mattresses.

  2. Walk lightly

    Keep foot traffic to a minimum until it's fully dry. If you must cross, clean socks — not shoes and not bare feet.

  3. Mind the transitions

    Damp carpet onto tile or floorboards is slippery. Take it steady at doorways, especially with kids and older family.

  4. Keep furniture off it

    Leave the protective tabs or blocks under the legs for a full 24 hours. Timber and metal feet can leach stain or rust marks into damp fibre.

The week after

Keeping it that way.

  • Vacuum a few days later. Cleaning loosens soil that works its way up as the pile dries. A vacuum after 2 to 3 days lifts the last of it.
  • Blot spills, don't rub. Press a clean white cloth straight down. Rubbing drives the spill into the backing and frays the pile.
  • Skip the supermarket spot cleaners. Most leave a sticky residue that attracts dirt, so the spot comes back darker than before. Water and blotting is safer.
  • Rotate rugs occasionally. Evens out sun fade and traffic wear.

A spot has reappeared. What now?

This is called wicking — a spill that soaked into the backing travels back up as the carpet dries. It's normal and usually fixable. Blot it lightly with a clean damp white cloth first. If it's still there, message us and we'll come back and sort it.
