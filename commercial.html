How we get around it

Low-moisture encapsulation. The cleaning agent crystallises around each soil particle instead of flooding the fibre. The crystals are then vacuumed away, so the carpet or seating takes on a fraction of the moisture and is dry, groomed and back in use within one to two hours.

That’s what makes a scheduled maintenance program realistic for a working venue. We clean around your trading hours, and nothing is roped off the next morning.

For a deeper restorative reset we still recommend hot water extraction once or twice a year, booked separately at a quiet point in your calendar.
