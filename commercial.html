How we work

Two ways to book us.

Scheduled maintenance

Recurring monthly or quarterly visits across all your upholstered seating. Quoted as one flat facility rate, discounted below ad-hoc pricing for locking in the schedule.

Same-day call-ups

Spill, stain or VIP checkout. Priority response with a fixed rush fee — no surprise multiplier, no guessing what it'll cost.

Maintenance and same-day work both use low-moisture encapsulation cleaning — dry and back in service within 1–2 hours, not the 6+ hours traditional hot water extraction needs. A periodic hot water extraction reset is available separately, annually or biannually, for a deeper restorative clean.
