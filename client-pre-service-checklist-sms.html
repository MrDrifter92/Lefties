Lefties Pre‑Service Notes
LeftiesLite

A quick heads‑up so everything goes smoothly on the day. No homework — just a few tips that help us get you the best result. 💙

Clear the little things

If there are small items or breakables in the cleaning area (toys, lamps, plants), moving them aside helps us start faster. We’ll handle the big stuff where safe.

Parking & access

A spot near the entry is perfect. We’ll need access to a power point and a tap.

Kids & pets

Once we’re set up, it’s safest if children and pets hang out in another room while hoses and equipment are in use.

Furniture
  • We’ll work around larger items or move them carefully where possible.
  • Want a specific area under/behind furniture cleaned? Just let us know.
Drying time

Surfaces may be lightly damp after cleaning. With airflow or fans, most areas dry within a few hours.

Questions or changes?

If anything changes (access, parking, extra items), a quick message is perfect and helps us plan.
