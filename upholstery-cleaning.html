Upholstery & Lounges

Revitalizing your favorite furniture to its pristine condition. From delicate fabrics to durable leathers, we provide a deep, hygienic clean that restores both comfort and style.

The Upholstery Process

  • 01

    Fabric Identification We assess fiber type to choose the safest chemistry for your specific piece.

  • 02

    High-Power Vacuum Removing grit, hair, and dust from every crevice before any water is applied.

  • 03

    Low-Moisture Prep Specialized pre-sprays break down oils and sweat without soaking the padding.

  • 04

    Fabric-Matched Rinse Hot water extraction or gentle low-moisture cleaning based on fabric needs.

  • 05

    Accelerated Drying High-airflow passes ensure most furniture is ready to use in just a few hours.

Why Choose Us?

  • ● No Franchises: We take the time needed for detail-oriented cleaning.
  • ● Ethical Advice: If a fabric is too fragile or a stain is permanent, we'll tell you upfront.
  • ● Child & Pet Safe: Residue-free, professional grade products that are safe immediately after drying.
  • ● Fast Recovery: Most upholstery is dry within 4-6 hours.
