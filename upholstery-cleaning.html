Upholstery & Lounges

We specialize in revitalizing your upholstery and lounges, bringing them back to their pristine condition. Whether your furniture is made of delicate fabric or durable leather, and regardless of whether it has grubby arms or food-stained cushions, we provide a deep clean. Our process ensures proper rinsing, leaving your upholstery fresh, hygienic, and looking its best.

What’s in the process?

  • Pre-check of fabric type (we clean natural & synthetic safely): We identify the fabric to choose the safest, most effective method.
  • Vacuum & prep to remove hair, grit, and surface grime: A thorough pre-vacuum removes loose soil and debris.
  • Low-moisture pre-spray to soften oils, sweat, and stains: Breaks down stubborn grime without over-wetting.
  • Rinsed via hot water extraction or safe low-moisture method: Both leave no sticky residue and restore freshness.
  • Drying pass to reduce moisture; ready within hours: Most pieces are ready to use in 4–6 hours.

What makes us different?

  • Local, not a franchise: More time and care on every job.
  • Ethical operators: Straight talk; if we can’t, we’ll say so and point you right.
  • Child & pet-safe: Non-toxic, professional-grade products that dry residue-free.
  • Optional add-ons: Odour treatments, sanitizers, and fabric protector on request.

How long does it take to dry?

Most upholstery is typically dry within 4–6 hours. Fabric, humidity, and airflow can nudge that up or down — we’ll help you set ventilation for a quick dry.

Need to know

  • Fully insured, police-checked, Blue Card: Peace of mind in your home.
  • WoolSafe compatible on delicate fabrics: Gentle and effective for sensitive fibres.
  • Honest stain advice: Some stains are permanent — we’ll set clear expectations upfront.
