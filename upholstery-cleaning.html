Upholstery & Lounge Revival

Fabric-safe clean. No harsh smells. Real results that feel fresh, not crunchy.

What we clean

Sofas, modulars, armchairs, recliners, dining chairs, ottomans, cushions.

How we do it

Fibre check, pre-vac, fabric-safe prespray, gentle agitation, thorough rinse or low-moisture clean, speed-dry.

Straight-up pricing

By seat. Fabric and condition decide the final. We confirm before we start. No gimmicks.

The process

  1. 1. Fibre ID & colour test
    Quick spot test on hidden area to set safe chemistry.
  2. 2. Dry soil removal
    Pre-vac to get the loose stuff out first.
  3. 3. Fabric-safe prespray
    Targeted mix for the fabric and the soil.
  4. 4. Gentle agitation
    Hand tools or soft pads where needed.
  5. 5. Rinse or low-moisture clean
    We choose the method that suits the fabric, not the other way around.
  6. 6. Speed-dry
    Air movers to kickstart dry time. We leave care tips.

Common fabrics

  • Microfibre
  • Polyester
  • Olefin/Polypropylene
  • Wool blends
  • Cotton/Linen blends
  • Viscose/Rayon* (handle with care)

What to know

Natural fibres

Viscose, linen and some cottons can brown or water-mark. We use low-moisture methods and set expectations upfront.

Pre-treated stains

Supermarket spotters can set stains. We’ll test and do our best without risking the fabric.

Pet odours

We treat the source where possible and neutralise odours properly. No heavy perfumes.

Aftercare

  • Dry time about 4–6 hours with airflow.
  • Use clean towels where heads rest until fully dry.
  • If a mark wicks back, blot lightly and message us.
