The process

How it's done.

  1. Fabric forensics

    We test fibre reaction and dye stability before any moisture is introduced.

  2. Encapsulated pre-spray

    Specialised chemistry lifts body oils and grime to the surface for extraction.

  3. Shear-dry extraction

    High-power suction removes soil with minimal moisture, keeping dry times to 4–6 hours.
