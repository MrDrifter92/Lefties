Upholstery & Lounges

We specialize in revitalizing your upholstery and lounges, bringing them back to their pristine condition. Whether your furniture is made of delicate fabric or durable leather, and regardless of whether it has grubby arms or food-stained cushions, we provide a deep clean. Our process ensures proper rinsing, leaving your upholstery fresh, hygienic, and looking its best.

What’s in the process?

  • Pre-check of fabric type (natural & synthetic): We identify the fabric to choose the safest, most effective method.
  • Vacuum & prep: Remove hair, grit, and surface grime before we wet the fabric.
  • Low-moisture pre-spray: Softens oils, sweat, and stains without over-wetting.
  • Hot water extraction or low-moisture rinse: Thorough flush with no sticky residues.
  • Drying pass: Reduces moisture so most lounges are ready within hours.

What makes us different?

  • Local, not a franchise: More time and care on each job.
  • Ethical operators: Straight talk. If it’s not doable, we’ll say so and point you right.
  • Child & pet-safe: Professional, non-toxic products that dry residue-free.
  • Optional add-ons: Odour removal, sanitiser, and protector available on request.

How long to dry?

Typically 4–6 hours depending on fabric and airflow. We’ll help you set windows/fans to speed it along.
