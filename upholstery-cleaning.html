Upholstery & Lounges

We specialize in revitalizing your upholstery and lounges, bringing them back to their pristine condition. Whether your furniture is made of delicate fabric or durable leather, and regardless of whether it has grubby arms or food-stained cushions, we provide a deep clean. Our process ensures proper rinsing, leaving your upholstery fresh, hygienic, and looking its best.

What’s in the process?

  • Pre-check of fabric type (we clean natural & synthetic safely): Before we begin, our experts meticulously identify your upholstery's fabric type…
  • Vacuum and prep to remove hair, grit, and surface grime: High-powered vacuuming to remove loose dirt and pet hair…
  • Low-moisture pre-spray to soften oils, sweat, and stains: Gentle pre-treatment to emulsify oils and stains…
  • Rinsed via hot water extraction or safe low-moisture method: Method matched to fabric; no sticky residue…
  • Drying pass to reduce moisture; ready within hours: Dedicated drying pass; typically ready in a few hours.

What makes us different?

  • Local, not a franchise: More time and care on every job.
  • Ethical operators: Honest advice, referrals when needed.
  • Child and pet-safe: Non-toxic products; residue-free when dry.
  • Optional Add-ons: Odour removal, sanitizer, fabric protector.

How long does it take to dry?

Most upholstery is typically dry within 4–6 hours. We’ll help with airflow for quicker results.

Need to know

  • Fully insured, police-checked, Blue Card.
  • WoolSafe compatible products for delicate fabrics.
  • Upfront about impossible stains—no false promises.
