Upholstery & Lounges

We specialize in revitalizing your upholstery and lounges, bringing them back to their pristine condition. Whether your furniture is made of delicate fabric or durable leather, and regardless of whether it has grubby arms or food-stained cushions, we provide a deep clean. Our process ensures proper rinsing, leaving your upholstery fresh, hygienic, and looking its best.

What’s in the process?

  • Fabric ID first: We identify the fabric so we can choose the safest, most effective method.
  • Vacuum & prep: High-powered pre-vac to lift hair, grit and dust.
  • Low-moisture pre-spray: Softens oils, sweat and marks without overwetting.
  • Rinse method matched to fabric: Hot water extraction or safe low-moisture clean.
  • Drying pass: Accelerated drying so most pieces are ready within hours.

What makes us different?

  • Local, not a franchise: More time on each job, better results.
  • Ethical operators: Straight talk; if we can’t, we’ll say so and refer.
  • Child & pet-safe: Professional products, residue-free when dry.
  • Optional add-ons: Odour removal, sanitiser, fabric protection on request.

How long does it take to dry?

Most upholstery is typically dry within 4–6 hours. Leather and synthetic blends often dry even quicker. We’ll help with ventilation and fan placement to speed things up.

Need to know

  • Fully insured, police-checked & Blue Card: Peace of mind in your home.
  • WoolSafe compatible products used on delicate fabrics.
  • No false promises: We’ll tell you upfront what can and can’t be done.
