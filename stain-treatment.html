Woopsie Daisy Stains

Life happens, and so do spills! At Lefties Carpet Cleaning, we understand that accidents are inevitable. Our specialized stain treatment services are designed to tackle those "uh-ohs" on your carpets and upholstery, from everyday spills to more challenging marks. We use professional-grade solutions and techniques to identify, classify, and treat a wide variety of stains, aiming to restore your surfaces to their best possible condition.

What’s in the process?

  • Stain ID and classification: We identify the stain type (protein, oil, dye, etc.) and surface so we can pick a safe, effective method.
  • Spot-specific solutions: Pro-grade formulas target the stain without harming fibres or dyes.
  • Gentle agitation + dwell: Worked in lightly and left to react for maximum lift.
  • Controlled extraction & rinse: Residue-free removal to prevent re-soiling.
  • Post-treatment & protection (optional): Extra steps for difficult stains and protective coatings on request.

What makes us different?

  • Local, not a franchise: More time, more care, better results.
  • Ethical operators: If we can’t fix it, we’ll tell you—and point you to someone who can.
  • Child & pet-safe: Professional products, residue-free when dry.
  • Best paired with a full clean: Keeps the finish even and the room fresh.

How long does it take to dry?

Spot-only treatments usually dry within 1–2 hours. If combined with a full clean, expect 4–6 hours, depending on fabric and ventilation. We’ll help optimise airflow to speed it up.
