The process

How it’s done.

  1. Stain ID and classification

    We identify the stain type (protein, oil, dye) and surface so we can pick a safe, effective method.

  2. Spot-specific solutions

    Pro-grade formulas target the stain without harming fibres or dyes.

  3. Gentle agitation + dwell

    Worked in lightly and left to react for maximum lift.

  4. Controlled extraction & rinse

    Residue-free removal to prevent re-soiling.

  5. Post-treatment & protection

    Optional extra steps for difficult stains and protective coatings on request.

Spot-only treatments usually dry within 1 to 2 hours. Combined with a full clean, expect 4 to 6 hours depending on fabric and ventilation.
