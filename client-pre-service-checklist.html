Before we arrive

Just a few things.

A quick to-do list before we turn up. Ticking these off means we get straight into the deep clean, which gives you the best possible result.

Home/Before we arrive

  1. Clear the decks

    Move small items and breakables out of the areas we're cleaning — pot plants, lamps, loose rugs, kids' toys, pet beds.

  2. A quick vacuum

    If you can run the vacuum beforehand, brilliant. It clears the surface fluff so our extraction can focus on the embedded soil underneath.

  3. Plan for pets and kids

    For everyone's safety, please have children and pets secured in a separate area, away from the cleaning zone and the equipment.

  4. Access and parking

    We need a tap and a power outlet. A clear parking spot as close to the entry as possible saves us dragging hose the length of the street.

  5. A note on furniture

    We can generally work around or carefully move larger items like lounges and coffee tables. We don't move very heavy or delicate things — pianos, full bookcases, antiques. If you're unsure about something, just flag it when we arrive.

Anything we should know about?

Old spills you've already treated, pet accidents, or a stain you've had a go at with a supermarket product — tell us beforehand. It genuinely changes how we approach the job, and knowing in advance gets you a better result.

What to do afterwards

See you soon.

Something come up? Ring or text and we'll rearrange, no dramas.
