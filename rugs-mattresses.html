Rug & Mattress Cleaning

Extend the life and freshness of your rugs and mattresses with Lefties Carpet Cleaning. Beyond just surface cleaning, our specialized services delve deep to remove embedded dirt, dust mites, allergens, and odours, ensuring a truly hygienic environment. From delicate area rugs to large mattresses, we use safe, non-toxic products and proven methods to protect your investments and promote a healthier living space.

What’s in the process?

  • Pre-inspection & material assessment: Identify material, construction and concerns to choose the safest method.
  • Deep vacuuming: Remove embedded dust, dander and mites before any wet process.
  • Targeted pre-treatment: Eco-friendly solutions for stains and heavy soiling.
  • Gentle clean & extraction: Low-moisture hot water extraction or delicate dry method as appropriate.
  • Sanitise & deodorise: Hygiene-first approach that tackles bacteria and odours.
  • Accelerated drying: Air movers and good ventilation—usually ready within hours.

What makes us different?

  • Local, not a franchise: More time, more care, better results.
  • Ethical operators: Straight talk; if we can’t do it, we’ll say so and point you right.
  • Child & pet-safe: Professional products, residue-free when dry.
  • Comprehensive allergen removal: Great for sensitive homes—less dust, dander and pollen.

How long does it take to dry?

Most rugs and mattresses dry within 4–8 hours, depending on thickness, material and airflow. We’ll help optimise ventilation to speed things up.
