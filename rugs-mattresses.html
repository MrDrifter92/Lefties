The process

How it’s done.

  1. Pre-inspection & material assessment

    Identify material, construction and concerns to choose the safest method.

  2. Deep vacuuming

    Remove embedded dust, dander and mites before any wet process.

  3. Targeted pre-treatment

    Eco-friendly solutions for stains and heavy soiling.

  4. Gentle clean & extraction

    Low-moisture hot water extraction or delicate dry method as appropriate.

  5. Sanitise & deodorise

    Hygiene-first approach that tackles bacteria and odours.

  6. Accelerated drying

    Air movers and good ventilation, usually ready within hours.

Most rugs and mattresses dry within 4 to 8 hours, depending on thickness, material and airflow. We'll help optimise ventilation to speed things up.
