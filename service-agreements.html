Our agreement

No fine print, just the straight story.

Two agreements — one residential, one commercial. Both written to be read, not to be skimmed past.

Home/Service Agreements
Residential

Residential agreement.

Thanks for trusting us to bring your place back to life. This isn't a scary legal contract — it's us being upfront about how we work, what we need from you, and what you can expect.

01 The job we'll do

We agree to perform the carpet and/or upholstery cleaning services we quoted you for, using professional equipment and the right chemicals for the job. The goal is a spotless revival, every time.

02 What we need from you

  • Access: A clear path to bring our gear in and out.
  • Space: Please move small furniture, breakables, toys and personal items from the areas being cleaned. We can help with larger items like sofas and tables, but we can't move heavy or delicate things such as pianos, entertainment units or antique furniture.
  • Power & water: A standard power outlet and a tap, preferably an outdoor one.
  • Safety: Please keep kids and pets in another part of the house, away from the work area and our equipment.

03 The price

The price is what we agreed on in your quote. If we arrive and find something that needs a different approach or extra work — a major stain that wasn't mentioned, for instance — we stop and talk to you first. No surprise charges, ever.

04 Paying the bill

For residential jobs, payment is due on the day, as soon as the job is done. Cash, debit or credit card on the spot. You get a receipt right then and there.

05 The reality of stains and damage

  • No guarantee: We'll do everything we can, but we can't guarantee 100% removal of every stain. Bleach spots, old dye stains, and long-standing pet urine can be permanent. We'll be honest about what to expect before we start.
  • Pre-existing conditions: Cleaning can reveal damage that dirt was hiding — worn fibres, sun fading, or delamination where the backing separates. We're not responsible for pre-existing issues that become visible after a proper clean.
  • DIY spotters: If you've used a supermarket spot cleaner or a home remedy, please tell us. Those chemicals can react with professional products and affect the final result.

06 A heads-up on certain fibres

Every carpet is different. Natural fibres like wool and cotton need special care. We're trained to clean them safely, but there's always a small risk of minor shrinkage or colour variation. We manage that with wool-safe, pH-neutral products and proper technique.

07 If something goes wrong

We treat your home like it's our own. In the unlikely event we cause damage, tell us immediately. Our liability is limited to the cost of the cleaning service provided.

By letting us start work, you agree to these terms. Let's get this revival started.

Commercial

Commercial agreement.

Thanks for choosing Lefties to keep your commercial space in top shape. This outlines our working agreement so we're all on the same page: clear expectations, and a great result.

01 The job we'll do

We agree to perform the carpet and/or upholstery cleaning services quoted, using professional equipment and the right chemicals for the job.

02 What we need from you

  • Access: Clear, safe access to the areas to be cleaned, as agreed.
  • Space: Areas clear of documents, electronics and personal effects.
  • Power & water: A standard power outlet and water supply.
  • Safety: The work area must be clear of staff and customers for the duration of the clean.

03 The price

As per the accepted quote. If the scope changes, we issue a variation for your approval before proceeding. No surprise charges, ever.

04 Invoicing and payment terms

  • Standard commercial jobs: One-off or infrequent cleans run on a 7-day account. The invoice is sent on completion and is payable within 7 calendar days.
  • Major contracts and ongoing service: Clients with a formal ongoing agreement — aged care, hotels, large offices — get 30-day account terms. Invoices are issued at month end and payable within 30 calendar days.

Late payments may attract a surcharge as outlined in your quote. All quotes must be formally accepted before work is scheduled.

05 The reality of stains and damage

  • No guarantee: We aim for the best result, but can't guarantee 100% removal of stains that are permanent in nature — dyes, bleach, long-term chemical spills.
  • Pre-existing conditions: We're not responsible for fibre wear, fading or backing separation that becomes more apparent after cleaning.

06 If something goes wrong

We operate with full care and professionalism. In the unlikely event of damage caused by our team, notify us immediately. Our liability is limited to the cost of the cleaning service provided.

By accepting our quote and allowing us to commence work, your business agrees to these terms. We look forward to working with you.

Questions about the terms?

Ring and ask. We'd rather explain it now than have it be a surprise later.
