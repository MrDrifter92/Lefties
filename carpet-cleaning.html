The process

How it’s done.

  1. Pre-inspection

    Thorough vacuuming and identification of carpet type and soiled areas.

  2. Pre-treatment

    Targeted formulas for stubborn spots and high-traffic lanes.

  3. Deep extraction

    Advanced hot water extraction or low-moisture cleaning options.

  4. Hand-spotting

    Technical intervention for any remaining individual stains.

  5. Grooming

    Pile restoration for a uniform finish and faster drying.
