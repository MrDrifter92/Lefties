Carpet Cleaning

At Lefties Carpet Cleaning, we specialize in bringing your carpets back to life, restoring their original beauty and freshness. Our comprehensive process ensuring a deep clean that removes dirt and allergens.

The Process

  • 01

    Pre-Inspection Thorough vacuuming and identification of carpet type and soiled areas.

  • 02

    Pre-Treatment Targeted formulas for stubborn spots and high-traffic lanes.

  • 03

    Deep Extraction Advanced hot water extraction or low-moisture cleaning options.

  • 04

    Hand-Spotting Technical intervention for any remaining individual stains.

  • 05

    Grooming Pile restoration for a uniform finish and faster drying.
