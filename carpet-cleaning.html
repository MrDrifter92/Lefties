Carpet Cleaning

At Lefties Carpet Cleaning, we specialize in bringing your carpets back to life, restoring their original beauty and freshness. Our comprehensive cleaning process ensures a deep clean that removes dirt, allergens, and stains, leaving your home healthier and more inviting. Whether it's a high-traffic area or a delicate rug, we have the expertise and equipment to deliver outstanding results.

What’s in the process?

  • Pre-inspection and commercial vacuum: Every cleaning begins with a thorough pre-inspection to identify carpet type, heavily soiled areas, and potential stains. We then use a powerful commercial-grade vacuum to remove dry soil, dust, and loose debris, preparing the carpet for deep cleaning.
  • Targeted pre-treatment for stains: Stubborn spots and high-traffic areas receive special attention with targeted pre-treatment solutions. These professional-grade formulas are designed to break down tough stains and embedded dirt, ensuring maximum removal during the main cleaning phase.
  • Hot water extraction or low-moisture clean: Depending on your carpet's material and condition, we utilize either advanced hot water extraction (steam cleaning) for a deep, penetrating clean that flushes out contaminants, or a safe, low-moisture method for delicate carpets or faster drying times.
  • Hand-spot treatment as needed: After the primary cleaning, our technicians meticulously inspect for any remaining spots. We apply specialized hand-spotting techniques and solutions to address individual stubborn stains, ensuring the best possible outcome.
  • Carpet grooming for a uniform finish: To restore the carpet's pile and ensure a uniform appearance, we gently groom the carpet after cleaning. This helps to lift the fibers, promoting faster drying and leaving your carpet looking freshly revitalized.
  • Fast drying (same-day in most cases): Our efficient cleaning methods and powerful extraction equipment are designed to minimize drying times. In most cases, your carpets will be dry and ready for use within the same day, reducing inconvenience and allowing you to enjoy your clean home sooner.

How long does it take to dry?

Our carpet cleaning process is designed for efficiency, ensuring your carpets are dry as quickly as possible. In most cases, carpets are dry and ready for normal use within the same day, typically within 4 to 6 hours. Factors such as carpet type, humidity levels, and air circulation can influence drying times. We will always advise on the best ventilation practices to help speed up the process.
